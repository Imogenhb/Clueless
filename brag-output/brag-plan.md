# Brag Plan: Clueless

## What is this app?
Clueless is a digital closet that answers “What to wear today?” with weather-aware picks from your own wardrobe — plus try-on and an AI stylist.

## The angle
Cher energy, modern product: the daily panic of getting dressed, solved by a stone-and-orange closet that already knows the weather. Not “AI fashion SaaS” — a specific daily ritual with the wink *As if!*

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant Outfit-weight line: **As if!** — then snap to the real product question **What to wear today?** Soft stone wash + orange accent pulse.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Daily Clueless Pick card: weather chip (`18°C · Partly cloudy`) + garment chips (tee, jeans, sneakers) popping in one by one
- Cursor taps **I’m wearing this** — button flips to success
- Closet grid flash: four wardrobe cards in the real card style
- Floating orange **Clueless stylist** FAB opening with “Hey! I’m your Clueless stylist…”

## Outro / punchline
Logo wordmark **Clueless** + tagline **Your digital closet. Daily picks, tuned to the weather.** Soft *As if!* wink in muted type.

## User flow worth showing
Open home → weather-tuned daily pick reveals → tap “I’m wearing this” (result beat). Closet + stylist as supporting highlights.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: playful Cher wink meets clean app-store product demo
- Interpretation: warm, readable, product-first; humor only from “As if!” and the dressing panic — no fake startup jargon

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#fafaf9`
- Accent: `#ea580c`
- Text: `#1c1917`
- Muted text: `#78716c`
- Surface / cards: `#ffffff` with border `#e7e5e4`
- Display font: Outfit
- Body font: DM Sans
- Strongest visual element: Daily pick hero card with weather pill + orange primary CTA

## Share copy (draft)
Built Clueless — a digital closet that answers “What to wear today?” with weather-aware picks. As if getting dressed should be this easy.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (~120 BPM, playful/professional)
- Music treatment: start near 0s, volume ~0.28–0.35, gentle fade under final logo (~1.2s)
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; strong cues near 8.02 / 12.02 / 17.02 for pick reveal, wearing-it payoff, and outro; beat grid ~0.5s for chip sequence
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; warm orange glow / card shadow breathe with RMS — no waveforms
- SFX posture: moderate; motion-matched UI clicks, soft card slides, one soft impact on logo
- Audio-coupled moments: As if! slam; garment chips sequential; button click; logo land
- Restraint rule: no chaotic glitch spam; keep clicks quiet under the bed
